About Me

Healthcare data that benefits society, not just spreadsheets

I'm Andre Weissmann — a data analyst with deep roots in healthcare operations and a genuine obsession with two things: health data and differential privacy. I believe that when data is analyzed with care, it can do more than inform decisions — it can improve lives.

My path into analytics started with curiosity and grew through real work. Five years in healthcare billing operations at Andres Medical Billing / EMS Management & Consultants gave me domain knowledge that most analysts don't have: I understand the data before it reaches the dashboard.

I've built projects across SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, and Excel — not as demos, but as genuine explorations. The portfolio you're looking at right now is the antidote to platforms that hide your work behind a login wall.
